- Subheadings: left aligned, 14 pt, bold, Times New Roman.
- Body: 12 pt Times New Roman, justified, 1.5 line spacing.
- Include detailed explanations, relevant diagrams and tables.
- Include source code under the corresponding task.
- Leave clearly labelled space for each actual screenshot.
